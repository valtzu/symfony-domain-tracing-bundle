This application is an online shop where customers check out a cart and place orders. A separate payments service charges those orders. During checkout, the shop prices the order lines from the product catalog and applies coupons and customer tiers to produce a quote. It then checks stock in inventory, screens the order for fraud, calculates shipping and charges the payment. Checkout can fail at any of these steps. The engineers of the shop read the traces to see why a checkout failed or how an order was priced. The main business terms are customers, customer tiers, products, order lines, prices, coupons, quotes, stock, fraud screening, shipping, payments, charges, orders and order status.
